## 3) The problem, stated honestly

### The reality
Real-time communication is “solved” by incumbents, but it is solved in a way that is costly for new interactions.

### Common friction people accept today
- Account walls before you can speak
- Forced identity patterns (email, phone, usernames, social graphs)
- App switching between chat, calls, streams, and AI tools
- Monetization that is either subscriptions, ads, or manual negotiation
- Hosting value that is difficult to price fairly without overhead

### What early-stage builders experience
When a product’s pull is not instantly obvious, teams compensate with explanations, then marketing, then sales. That is expensive, slow, and not sustainable for small teams.

Meetred is designed to reduce the need for “explainers” by making the first experience immediately usable.

## 4) What Meetred is

Meetred is an always-on public room plus a room system:
- Public Room: global chat and discovery
- Direct Messages: peer-to-peer conversations
- Call Rooms: private two-way audio/video
- Stream Rooms: host live video with chat
- AI Rooms: group chat with a shared AI connected by the host

Everything runs in the browser with minimal onboarding friction.

Meetred’s first 10 seconds are designed to be the differentiator: you arrive in a live space, not a sign-up flow.

Instead of “channels vs calls vs streams vs AI,” Meetred turns them into one mental model: rooms for different activities.

### 4) Shared AI rooms are collaborative, not solitary
The AI room is not “you chatting with ChatGPT.” It is “a room chats with the same AI together,” which changes the dynamic for teams, communities, and live sessions.

Meetred is built for people who host value: conversations, streams, expertise, AI access, and time-based content.

### 6) User-controlled identity
Identity is browser-native and can be rotated, backed up, and restored without a traditional account system.

## 8) Monetization model

### Design principle
Monetization should map to usage. Pay for time used, not bundles.

### Primary revenue streams
- Pay-per-minute sessions for Call, Stream, and AI rooms (usage-based)
- Tips and one-off contributions to hosts
- Pay-per-view and time-based file viewing (for hosted content)
- Platform fee on paid sessions (small take rate)

### Why this is defensible
Most incumbents monetize through ads, subscriptions, or platform lock-in. Meetred monetizes hosting time and usage, which aligns incentives with both hosts and participants.

### Payments stack direction
- Stripe for mainstream payments
- Crypto rails where useful
- x402 planned to make pay-per-use feel web-native

## 13) Architecture overview (non-technical)

Meetred is browser-native real-time communication:
- WebRTC powers live audio/video
- Peer networking supports discovery and presence
- Identity is generated in the browser and can be backed up or rotated
- Some coordination services may exist for reliability, but the product is designed to minimize account dependence and maximize instant participation

## 14) Technical deep dive (for developers and researchers)

### Core components
- Web app: Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind + UI components
- Real-time media: WebRTC
- Peer networking: libp2p (WebRTC transport where applicable)
- Identity: browser-generated keys, optional backup/restore
- Signaling and coordination: designed to support low friction and reliability, with a roadmap toward more p2p-native coordination
- AI connections:
  - LM Studio local endpoint integration
  - OpenAI API integration via user-provided keys

### Design tradeoffs (framed positively)
- Meetred prioritizes instant access and usability over forcing a full decentralized setup on every user.
- The system is built to progressively reduce central reliance where feasible without harming user experience.

### Safety and moderation direction
- Room-level controls
- Rate limiting and abuse prevention
- Optional safety filtering for AI rooms
- Reporting and block mechanisms

## 15) Trust and safety FAQ (user-readable)

### Is Meetred safe?
Meetred is designed to minimize onboarding friction while still supporting safety controls. Users should treat public spaces as public, and private rooms as private. We are actively improving moderation and abuse prevention as we move from MVP to beta.

### Do you store my DMs?
Meetred is designed so private conversations do not automatically become permanent archives. DM history behavior is intentionally different from traditional messengers and is built around peer presence and user control. We will clearly document what is stored where and provide safe defaults.

### Do I need a wallet?
No. You can use Meetred without a wallet. Monetization features are designed to be optional and accessible through standard payments and crypto rails.

### What is Identity?
Identity is browser-native, auto-generated, and user-controlled. You can rotate it, and you can back it up and restore it later.
