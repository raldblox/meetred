# Start here

Welcome to Meetred.

Meetred is a live public room where you can chat and share your own spaces: a stream room, a call room, and an AI room. Everything runs in your browser.

## Get started in 60 seconds

1. You land in the public room.
2. Say hi, or open someone’s stream, call, or AI card.
3. Use the Stream, Call, or AI buttons next to the message box to post your own room card.
4. Invite a friend with a link.
5. Back up your identity if you want continuity on this device and across devices.

## What those buttons do

Stream, Call, and AI do not create a brand new room each time. They share your personal rooms by posting a room card to the public room.
