# Payments

Meetred is built for hosting: streaming, calls, and AI rooms.

Payments features are being introduced gradually.

## Paid minutes

Paid minutes lets a host charge for time spent in a session. The goal is simple: people pay for what they use, and hosts get paid for the time they spend hosting.

## Tips

Tips are one-time payments that viewers or participants can send to a host to show support.

## Transparency

When payments are involved, Meetred should always show clear pricing and receipts. If anything feels unclear in the UI, please report it as feedback.
