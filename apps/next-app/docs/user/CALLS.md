# Calls

Your call room is your personal space for two-way audio and video.

## Starting a call

1. Go to your Call page.
2. Share your call link to someone in a DM, or post a call card in the public room.
3. Join the room and enable camera and mic if you want.

## Joining a call

1. Open the call card or call link.
2. Allow camera and mic permissions if you want to use them.
3. Join the room.

## A simple privacy rule

A call room is link-based. Anyone with the link can try to join, so share links intentionally.
