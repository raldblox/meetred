# FAQ

## What is Meetred?

Meetred is a live public room where people chat and share links to streams, calls, and AI rooms. Everything runs in the browser.

## Do I need an account?

No. Meetred gives you an identity automatically when you open the app. You can back it up if you want to reuse it later.

## What are room cards?

Room cards are invite messages in the public room. Click a card to open the host’s stream, call, or AI room.

## What do the Stream, Call, and AI buttons in chat do?

They post a room card to the public room that links to your personal room. People can click the card to join you.

## Are DMs private?

DMs are private between you and the person you message. Meetred is designed so DM history lives on your devices, not as a central inbox.

## Why is a stream or AI room sometimes offline?

Streams and AI rooms run while the host is online. If the host closes the tab or disconnects, the room goes offline.

## Is Meetred safe?

Meetred is built with privacy-first defaults and minimal central storage. You still control what you share and who you interact with. Read Safety for best practices.

## Does Meetred record calls or streams?

Meetred does not record by default. As with any communication app, another participant can still record their own screen.

## Where can I send feedback?

If you see a feedback button in the app, use it. Otherwise, share feedback through the project’s public channels.
