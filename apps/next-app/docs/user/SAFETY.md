# Safety

Meetred is designed with privacy-first defaults and minimal central storage, so you can connect without heavy onboarding.

This page explains what to expect and how to use Meetred confidently.

## What is public vs private

- Public room messages are visible to everyone connected.
- DMs are private between you and the person you message.
- Stream chat is scoped to the stream room.
- Call and AI rooms are link-based invites. Share links intentionally.

## Identity safety

Your identity lives in your browser. Back it up if you want to reuse it across devices.

If you export a backup, store it somewhere safe. Anyone with your backup can act as you.

## Room links

Room links are powerful because they are simple. Treat them like invitations.
Share them with people you trust.

## Recording and screenshots

Meetred does not record by default. As with any live app, a participant can still record their own screen or save what they see. Share thoughtfully.

## A good default mindset

Use the public room like a public space, and private rooms like invitations. If something feels off, leave the room and rotate identity.
