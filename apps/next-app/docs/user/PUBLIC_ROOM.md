# Public room

The public room is Meetred’s main lobby. Everyone who opens Meetred lands here.

## What you can do

- Chat with everyone in the room.
- Open room cards shared by others.
- Post your own room card so others can join your stream, call, or AI room.

## Posting your room card

The buttons next to the message box post your personal room card to the public room:
- Stream posts your stream room card.
- Call posts your call room card.
- AI posts your AI room card.

This is how people discover what you are hosting.

## Is the public room private?

No. The public room is public.

Use it like a public forum. Avoid sharing sensitive personal details or anything you would not post publicly.
