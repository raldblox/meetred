# Direct messages

Direct messages are private chats between you and another identity.

## Quick answer

Your DM history is stored on the devices in that conversation. Meetred is designed to avoid keeping a central server inbox for your DMs.

This is a privacy advantage. It also means your history depends on your device and identity.

## What to expect

- If you use the same device and identity, your DM thread should feel consistent.
- If you switch devices or clear site data, you may not see older messages.
- When both sides are online, the app can sync recent messages more easily.

## How to keep DM history

- Back up your identity if you plan to use Meetred across devices.
- Avoid clearing site data if you want to keep local history.
- If you are on a shared device, consider rotating identity when you are done.

If you want to understand why identity matters, read Identity.
