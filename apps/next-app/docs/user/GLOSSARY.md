# Glossary

Public room: the global chat everyone lands in.

Room card: an invite message in the public room that links to a stream, call, or AI room.

Room link: a link that opens a stream room, call room, or AI room.

Host: the person who owns a room and controls when it starts.

Viewer: a person watching a stream.

DM: a private direct message conversation between identities.

Identity: your Meetred identity stored in your browser.

Back up identity: exporting your identity so you can reuse it later.

Rotate identity: creating a new identity for a fresh start or extra privacy.

Stream room: a host-only broadcast room with stream chat.

Call room: a two-way audio and video room.

AI room: a room where the host connects an AI model and others chat with it.
