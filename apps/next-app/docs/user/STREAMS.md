# Streams

Your stream room is your personal space to broadcast. Viewers can watch and chat.

## For hosts

1. Go to your Stream page.
2. Start your stream when you are ready.
3. Share your stream by posting it to the public room or sending the link in a DM.

Only the host can broadcast in a stream room.

## For viewers

1. Click a stream card in the public room, or open a stream link.
2. Watch and chat in the stream room.

## Stream chat

Stream chat is separate from public chat. Messages in stream chat stay in that stream room.

## If the stream ends

Streams run while the host is online. If the host disconnects or closes the tab, the stream ends.
