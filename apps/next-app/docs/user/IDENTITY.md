# Identity

Meetred gives you an identity automatically when you open the app.

Think of identity as your seat in Meetred. It controls your name, your DMs, and how others recognize you.

## Where your identity lives

Your identity is stored locally in your browser. This is why Meetred can work without signups.

## Back up your identity

Back up your identity if you want continuity across devices or if you want to avoid losing your identity after clearing browser data.

Keep your backup safe. Anyone with your exported identity can act as you.

## Rotate identity

Rotating identity creates a new identity. Use this if you want a fresh start or extra privacy.

Rotating identity can reset continuity, including how others recognize you and your local DM history.
